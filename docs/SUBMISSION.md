# Submission Strategy

## One-line pitch

Every failed run becomes a teammate. Record your movement, reset the timeline, and cooperate with echoes of your past self to escape a collapsing experiment.

## Judging alignment

### Playability

- One-screen tutorial with three explicit steps.
- Familiar movement controls and a single SHIFT action.
- First successful paradox can be solved in under three minutes.

### Originality

- Past runs are not decorative ghosts; their final and replayed positions alter live level logic.
- Puzzles are authored around choreography between present and past selves.

### Codex collaboration

- Core timeline model developed test-first.
- Codex assisted implementation, QA automation, visual inspection, and bug correction.
- Development decisions and division of responsibility are preserved in `CODEX_LOG.md`.

### Release potential

- Static browser deployment with no login barrier.
- A complete 36-round campaign is organized into six chapters with persisted local progress.
- The data-driven level contract supports authored expansion, daily challenges, and future user-generated timelines.
- Mobile controls are included from the first vertical slice.
- Hive integration can later provide identity, leaderboards, analytics, and live challenges.

### Presentation

- High-contrast diagnostic visual identity.
- Mechanic is readable from a short gameplay video.
- Player, echo, locked door, open door, and exit each use distinct colors.

## Scope guard

The 36-round core campaign is implemented. Before submission, prioritize direct balance, sound, deployment, and presentation rather than adding:

- generative dialogue,
- inventory or crafting,
- online multiplayer,
- procedural level generation,
- cinematic cutscenes,
- backend account requirements.
